03 — Results

HT Reel calculates a video bitrate from your target size, duration, and audio settings, then encodes once — this is fast but approximate, so results typically land within about 5-10% of target (a safety margin is built in to bias toward staying under, not over). Encoding runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly; nothing is uploaded anywhere, but that also means it's slower than server-side tools and works best on shorter clips or moderate resolutions. If a video is already smaller than your target and doesn't need format/trim/resize changes, it's left completely untouched.
