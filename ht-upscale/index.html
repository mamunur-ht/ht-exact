HT Upscale — AI image enlargement

Bigger, and actually sharper.

Enlarge photos with an AI super-resolution model running entirely in your browser — new detail is reconstructed, not just stretched. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The AI-strength control blends the AI result with a plain high-quality upscale: slide it down for results that stay true to the original. Model and engine download once (~5 MB) and are cached by the browser after that.

01 — Add images

Click to choose files or drag them in, or paste (Ctrl+V) — PNG, JPG, WEBP, AVIF. Select, drop, or paste several at once to upscale them all together.

02 — Set options

0% = plain crisp resize 50%

AI upscaling is tuned for photos — on screenshots and small text it can add fake strokes that look cartoonish, so 50% is the default. Slide AI strength down (25–50%) to keep the output true to the original; 0% gives a plain crisp resize. AI runs in a background worker, so the page stays responsive.

03 — Results

HT Upscale runs the Real-ESRGAN x4-v3 super-resolution model through onnxruntime in WebAssembly — the model and engine download once and are cached by the browser after that. The AI-strength control blends the AI result with a plain high-quality upscale, keeping output true to the original — ideal for screenshots and small text. Images are processed in 256×256 tiles so even large photos fit comfortably in memory; bigger images take longer (a 1024×1024 photo at 4× with default strength is roughly a minute on a typical laptop). Output is capped at 8192 px on the long side; anything larger would exceed browser canvas limits. Transparent PNGs are flattened onto a white background, since the model works in RGB. All processing happens locally in your browser; files are never sent anywhere.
